# Meowthjr's legit menu (Knife Duels)

A local-only cosmetic menu for the Roblox game **Knife Duels**. Everything it changes is on your own screen only:
your equipped knife, your character and what other players see are never touched, and nothing is sent to the server.

Made by meowthjr & Claude.

## Load it

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emmauwuuwu/knife-duels-menu/main/KnifeDuelsMenu.lua"))()
```

## Features

- **Any knife in your hands:** pick from every knife in the game, even ones you don't own. It shows with that knife
  type's real first-person animations (equip, attacks, inspect, throw). The game's own view model factory builds it,
  and it plays whatever the game plays on its own view model.
- **Thrown and stuck knives** show the knife you picked, on your screen.
- **Tracers** behind your thrown knives (they fade out properly after a hit or headshot) and along the blade when you
  swing. Only on your own knife.
- **Tint, spin, knife position, Shuffle, favorites,** and knives sorted by rarity.
- **Boost:** more FPS (graphics quality, FPS cap, lighter world). Fog, haze, smoke and particles are left alone.
- **Display, World, Camera, Settings:** stretched resolution, overlay, self highlight, color looks, motion blur, FOV,
  hotkeys, window size, accent color.

**RightShift** shows or hides the window, and **RightCtrl** frees the cursor. Both keys can be changed in Settings.
